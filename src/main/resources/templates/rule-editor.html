How matching works

An address or @domain entry must match. Any included subject phrase qualifies; leave the list empty to match every subject. Any exception phrase prevents the rule from acting. Use plain letters, numbers and spaces in subject phrases. Gmail domain and subject previews use Gmail search. Outlook domain rules use sender-address text matching; subject conditions match text within the subject.

Saving a draft or preview creates no destination. A new Outlook folder is created at the top level. Manual filing and other rules may also contribute to destination results.
