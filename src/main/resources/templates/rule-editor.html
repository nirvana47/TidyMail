How matching works

A listed sender must match. Any included subject phrase qualifies; leave the list empty to match every subject. Any exception phrase prevents the rule from acting. Use plain letters, numbers and spaces in subject phrases. Gmail previews check phrase matches with Gmail search; Outlook matches text within the subject.

Saving a draft or preview creates no destination. A new Outlook folder is created at the top level. Manual filing and other rules may also contribute to destination results.
